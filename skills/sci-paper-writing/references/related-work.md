## Related Work rules

Help readers locate this paper on the relevant research lines, identify its closest predecessors and understand the actual differences. Classify to support that positioning, not to produce a miniature field survey. Follow the existing search/content-verification and bibliography routes; prestige, citation count or a reference template's wording is not evidence for a literature claim.

### Division with Introduction

| 内容 | Introduction | Related Work |
|---|---|---|
| 背景与问题 | 使问题成立，说明研究必要性 | 通常不重铺背景 |
| 已有研究 | 主要路线及与动机有关的挑战 | 路线间的机制、条件及有意义的差异 |
| 本文定位 | 为什么需要这一研究思路 | 与哪些工作最接近，具体改变了什么 |
| 技术细节 | 仅保留理解动机所需的信息 | 仅保留支持比较所需的信息，完整实现仍在 Method |
| 性能与贡献 | 有证据的贡献概括 | 不展开性能排名，不重列全部贡献或 Method 总览 |

When a separate Related Work is chosen, use the introduction reference's shortened route overview and move detailed comparisons here while preserving the challenge's necessary causal premises. The two sections may share a classification spine or cite the same paper for different supported purposes; do not copy paragraphs or repeat the same claim at the same granularity. Without an independent Related Work, keep the approved integrated introduction review rather than adding a second one. Confirm a material manuscript reorganization under the existing logic-design gate; maintaining this skill does not authorize moving live paper text.

### Classification and scope

Usually organize **2–3 research lines or themes**, adapting to actual relationships. Choose a useful organizing dimension: object of adaptation (input/features/predictions/structures), core strategy, information/supervision/access conditions, or a research question directly relevant to this paper. Keep a taxonomy's levels coherent; an optimization strategy, application and backbone architecture are not automatically mutually exclusive categories.

Different themes may legitimately overlap. Distinguish parallel research topics from an exhaustive partition, and explain the basis instead of asserting that every paper belongs to exactly one class. A paper may appear in multiple themes for different reasons without receiving its full description again. Subsection names normally describe the literature topic, not this paper's newly named modules. Do not force topic count, manufacture a class or omit the nearest competitor to improve the novelty narrative.

### Chinese paragraph and sentence blueprint

A separate Related Work usually targets **400–600 English words**, roughly **3–5 substantive paragraphs across 2–3 themes**, shorter when sufficient. These are flexible suggestions, not measured reference lengths, journal limits or quotas; a verified venue requirement and confirmed project plan take priority. Optional opening scope is **0–30 words**, each substantive paragraph about **80–130 words**, and an optional final positioning statement **0–50 words**. The nearest-work comparison usually belongs inside the relevant paragraph, not another obligatory summary. Allocate within the total rather than summing every upper bound or padding short content.

| 句子功能 | 中文内容逻辑 |
|---|---|
| 确立共同思路 | 研究线通过共同操作处理共同对象或问题，由对应文献支撑 |
| 展开一支 | 一部分工作利用某种信号或机制实现具体能力，选有代表性的文献说明 |
| 呈现实质差别 | 另一部分在相同维度改变处理，以应对不同条件或需要 |
| 综合已有进展 | 概括真正共享的能力与适用条件，避免将部分论文的限制推广给整类 |
| 按需定位本文 | 紧接最接近工作，在同一对象/条件上说明真实差异及其含义 |

These are selectable roles, not five mandatory sentences. A paragraph may simply establish a research line; not every category needs a defect or a “however” ending. When the paragraph establishes a limitation relevant to the paper, state its mechanism, conditions and task consequence clearly, then explain the corresponding contribution. Preserve justified criticism and contrasts rather than reducing the passage to a neutral description of the proposed method. Ordinary chronological history is useful only when it explains a consequential change in ideas, not as the default organizing order.

### Nearest-work comparison and non-list prose

Use **共同问题或基础 → 决定性差别 → 差别改变了什么** as a content check. Compare the same dimension, such as processing object, available information, representation granularity, constraint content, training/inference stage or use conditions. Keep an operation's object, level, criterion and assumptions distinct. Descriptions that share a broad topic are not automatically equivalent; establish their relationship from the actual formulation before replacing one with another. Differences can concern goals, assumptions or cost rather than defects. A short difference statement can end once the content makes the positioning clear; no generic superiority sentence is needed.

Make the information unit a shared proposition or relationship among works. Group papers that genuinely support a common strategy, then explain the meaningful branches and inspect the closest work in more detail. A foundational paper, direct baseline or closest predecessor may deserve an individual sentence; avoid giving every retrieved paper one equally weighted “author proposed X” sentence.

Do not equate no explicit modeling with total inability, turn a few examples into an all-methods failure claim, or claim novelty merely from renamed concepts or a module combination. If prior work already covers an idea, identify the real incremental difference at the appropriate granularity. Unsupported negative claims must be narrowed or verified rather than used to manufacture a gap. No fixed citation count, year quota or exhaustive-coverage claim is implied by this structure.

Group citations only when they support the same proposition under the stated conditions; attach specific mechanisms to identifiable sources. Read and verify the relevant original passage under the existing fourteen search rules. Do not use citation volume as a substitute for comparison or import another paper's references without checking their actual support.

### Implicit flow and paragraph comments

Let content carry the transition: one processing object to another, a strategy to its information requirements, a broad family to the closest work, or two research lines meeting at a shared object. These are possible relationships, not a fixed order, a forced defect progression or an empty “another important direction” bridge.

Use the local plan's research-positioning field for the topic, comparison dimension and Introduction division; put representative keys and source support in its citation-verification field. Follow the canonical comment ownership and evidence rules.
