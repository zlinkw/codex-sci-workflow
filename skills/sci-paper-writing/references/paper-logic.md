## Select and inspect references

Identify the actual manuscript, reply and reference documents. Reuse already supplied material; ask for genuinely missing preferences/examples in one concise batch. One favorite paper or passage is enough for a provisional profile; do not require three samples or repeat intake every round. Ask what is valued: argument, tone, brevity, sentence structure, experiments or presentation. Distinguish the author's own passages from coauthored/external examples.

Read the relevant original passage, not just its filename, abstract or search snippet. Record its exact locator and intended role. Compare article type, audience, task conditions, study design and evidence strength before borrowing a structure. A prestigious or topically similar paper is not automatically a suitable pattern. An inaccessible source remains an explicit gap. Pick the closest reference at the relevant level rather than forcing one paper's style onto every section.

## Implicit paragraph flow: explicit in comments, economical in prose

The confirmed preference is to let readers infer connections from well-organized content with minimum sufficient overt cues. It does not hide scientific premises, uncertainty or qualifications. Apply these techniques where the actual relation supports them:

1. **Concept relay:** carry the same concrete object, mechanism or unresolved property into the next paragraph's topic without repeating a whole summary.
2. **Content-bearing contrast:** let different conditions, scales, mechanisms or criteria expose a real contrast. Use a short connective when otherwise ambiguous; do not manufacture causality between parallel approaches.
3. **Problem–solution echo:** repeat the precise challenge concept in its actual solution; term correspondence must match the implemented mechanism and evidence.
4. **Dual-purpose boundaries:** an opening/closing sentence advances substantive information while connecting passages. Remove empty recap/announcement bridges, retaining a premise when the inference needs it.

Record the previous established premise, the local new information/relation and onward handoff in the selected local plan fields. Do not translate these planning comments into explanatory prose. Read neighboring paragraphs together: the carried concept should be identifiable, the relationship justified and the new paragraph informative. Do not force variety in connectors, a defect at every boundary or a final effectiveness sentence. In replies, implicit flow never substitutes for a direct answer.

## Introduction opening: background serving the problem

The author's principle is **“一般是先有问题，再自然而然地导出背景。”** Select background because it makes the actual problem, cause, consequence or research need intelligible. This is problem-led planning, not a requirement to put a formulaic problem sentence first. Use the detailed [Introduction blueprint](introduction.md#opening-choose-background-from-the-problem); customize sentence roles from the inspected reference and current material. Remove detached definitions, historical tours and field praise. A short contextual start is useful only when it directly supplies the premise for the concrete problem.

## Logic-design discussion gate

For a new paper, explicit whole-paper logic confirmation or material redesign, inspect supplied references AND current manuscript/material before proposing organization. Analyze:
- sentence-level propositions, assumptions, cause/effect, contrast, qualifiers and evidence attachment;
- sentence-to-sentence known/new information, referents, inherited premises and justified inference;
- paragraph questions, new content, order, duplication and handoff;
- section-level problem → design → testable expectation → evidence → supported answer;
- the whole-paper central question, real innovation, competing explanations, evidence and boundaries.

Build a concise proposed argument map rooted in the actual task/material. Explain what transfers from references and what must change. Ask a small batch of concrete questions on genuine unresolved branches; recommend options and discuss consequential tradeoffs, without a generic interview or inventing reviewer intent/results.

Obtain the author's organization decision before full drafting/restructuring. Persist the confirmed compact chain in the main top profile under the canonical contract, together with relevant terms and budget; keep all local reasoning at its source. If facts are missing, identify them rather than create the argument from a remembered template. During scoped edits, check affected links only; reopen a settled plan only for a substantive contradiction or new evidence.

## Response-letter template mechanism

Inspect the reference's opening, reviewer/editor hierarchy, quotation style, response heading, acknowledgments, argument sequence, revised-text quotation, location statement and closing. Build a small internal courtesy palette at the author's preferred formality, including short per-item thanks if selected. Use the canonical response guide for substance, length and status rather than copying those rules here.

Adapt to the actual concern: clarification, correction, new evidence, scope or justified disagreement. A reused courtesy sentence must not imply agreement, an improvement or an experiment that did not occur. Learn the pattern while writing an accurate direct answer tied to the actual manuscript.
