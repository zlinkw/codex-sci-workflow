# Discussion writing

Entry guard: apply the active [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) kernel and its real top/local-comment gate first. If the kernel is absent, read it once; this backlink does not rerun routing. Other links activate only for an actual task condition, never recursive preload.

Keep the section roles distinct: **实验结果报告观察与对照；Discussion 综合解释贡献、意义与边界；Conclusion 给出最终研究答案；摘要独立概括整项研究。** A discussion sentence that points back to a result should add interpretation, significance or scope rather than repeat a table. Conclusions and abstracts must agree scientifically without reusing the abstract's background-to-module-list-to-numbers narrative. Apply the existing evidence, citation and implicit-flow rules rather than creating another evidence ledger.

### Discussion: four flexible paragraph functions

The author's default is about **280–400 English words**, with these adaptable functions. The ranges are suggested budgets, not measured reference lengths, venue limits or quotas. Verified venue requirements and a confirmed project blueprint take priority; merge/split functions as the real argument requires and do not pad to a target or require an independent theoretical, clinical or future-work paragraph.

| 段落功能 | 段内句子逻辑 | 建议英文词数 |
|---|---|---:|
| 回答核心问题 | 核心发现及成立条件 → 回应的研究问题 → 增加的认识；直接回答引言，不重铺背景或重列模块 | 45～65 |
| 解释关键结果 | 最有解释价值的现象 → 与机制对应的证据 → 合理解释 → 必要的解释边界 | 90～130 |
| 提炼意义 | 相对已有研究改变的条件、认识或能力 → 对任务的具体价值 → 成立范围或代价 | 65～100 |
| 克制交代边界 | 当前实现或验证范围 → 尚未确定的相关扩展条件 → 对应验证或改进方向 | 60～90 |

Develop the one or two phenomena most informative about the contribution, not one discussion paragraph per module or a second account of every ablation. Combine relevant error changes, meaningful replacement controls and training/mechanism evidence when available. Distinguish an observed effect from a supported interpretation and from an untested causal account; a gain alone cannot establish the intended mechanism. Consider a consequential alternative explanation when the evidence leaves it open, without inventing an exhaustive counterargument list.

Make significance concrete: what information, annotation or access requirement was reduced; what previously unconstrained capability was supported; which condition affecting adaptation was clarified; or what benefit was obtained at what verified cost. Compare relevant prior evidence under its actual assumptions rather than repeating rankings or starting another literature survey. Methodological insight can be sufficient; do not inflate it into theoretical proof, universal generalization or clinical effectiveness without matching evidence.

### Limitations: proportionate, specific boundaries

Prioritize real implementation/validation boundaries relevant to the claims. Do not enumerate hypothetical severe failures, turn an untested setting into an observed failure, import a reference paper's weakness, or list generic field-wide problems merely to fill a limitations paragraph. Select the relevant boundaries without a fixed count. Explain their actual scope and impact rather than using vague self-criticism or empty reassurance.

| 边界类型 | 可以说明什么，前提是符合当前研究事实 | 不能据此跳到什么结论 |
|---|---|---|
| 验证范围 | 已在哪些任务与条件下验证；相关扩展还需确认什么 | 未测试其他场景就断言泛化能力差 |
| 当前实现 | 当前维度、分辨率或计算方式；扩展涉及什么条件 | 实现范围有限就否定核心方法 |
| 效果与成本 | 实测收益、实际成本及有依据的优化空间 | 缺乏代价证据却宣称适合部署 |

Use **已验证范围内的结论 → 尚未覆盖的具体条件 → 有针对性的验证/改进方向** as a planning aid, not mandatory stock sentences. When existing experiments bound an issue's impact, cite that evidence and its tested interval; without such evidence, do not append “this does not affect our conclusions.” Keep observed failures and untested conditions distinct. A known issue that changes the main conclusion requires correcting the claim or addressing the evidence gap, not relabeling it as harmless future optimization. Preserve adverse evidence required by the experimental rules, while avoiding redundant narration of minor details already adequately reported.

Future directions should follow a real boundary or unresolved question. Name what must be confirmed or changed, instead of ending with an unqualified list of more data, more scenarios and more research. A direction is not a completed result or an automatic promise to perform new experiments. No separate future-work paragraph is required.

### Implicit flow and local comment persistence

Let a carried object/condition connect the argument: a core finding leads to its informative conditions, those conditions help interpret a mechanism, that interpretation establishes concrete significance, and the significance is bounded by its requirements. This is a possible relationship, not a fixed order or a reason to insert a bridge at each boundary. For instance, a discussion of structural information can lead directly to its availability under different acquisition conditions; no generic concession sentence is needed.

Use the local plan's discussion/conclusion field for interpretation, evidence, added meaning and scope; distinguish observed failure from untested conditions. Apply the canonical comment ownership and affected-scope checks.
