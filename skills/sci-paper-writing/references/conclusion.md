Conclusion gives the final supported research answer and its scope. It agrees scientifically with the abstract and Results without repeating their narrative. Discussion rules are needed only when actually revising that section.

### Conclusion: one compact final answer

Default to **one paragraph of about 80–120 English words**, usually three or four content-bearing sentences, shorter when sufficient. These are flexible suggestions, not quotas or journal requirements.

| 句子功能 | 中文内容逻辑 |
|---|---|
| 最终回答 | 在研究条件下，核心思路回应了实际问题；重点是研究答案，无须重新列出方法全名和所有模块 |
| 依据与范围 | 关键验证支持了最重要的能力或认识；保留必要的任务、条件与不确定性限定 |
| 贡献落点 | 为具体研究或使用问题提供了已被支持的改进或思路，而非泛称重大意义 |
| 可选收尾 | 仅在必要时点明与前文一致的扩展方向或关键边界，不强制以未来工作结尾 |

Do not reintroduce background, define acronyms again, give a contribution list or reproduce a group of benchmark numbers. Retain a number only when indispensable to the final judgment and already verified. Add no new result, first-time scientific explanation or untested generalization; place any needed new evidence and verified contextual citation in the appropriate preceding section first. Avoid unsupported claims such as a new era or enormous clinical potential. If a boundary determines whether the conclusion holds, retain the qualifier without copying the entire limitations paragraph. The same core conclusion may recur across abstract and conclusion; scientific consistency is required, textual/narrative duplication is not.
