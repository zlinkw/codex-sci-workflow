# Abstract writing

Entry guard: apply the active [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) kernel and its real top/local-comment gate first. If the kernel is absent, read it once; this backlink does not rerun routing. Other links activate only for an actual task condition, never recursive preload.

The author's default abstract is a sequence of functional blocks, not a fixed sentence count: **任务与应用必要性 → 关键挑战及后果 → 自己的方法 → 实验验证与代表性结果 → 代码声明（适用时）**. The challenge block preserves the task's established background and faithfully compresses the introduction's supported limitation-and-design argument. When the gap is derived from the strategies used by existing methods, retain that premise and the reasoning leading to the limitation; do not replace it with a detached verdict about the task. Preserve the author's valid rhetorical structure while correcting the specific scientific wording. Do not replace background with requirements invented for the proposed method, or remove a genuine research gap merely to avoid strong wording. Preserve meaning, scope and consequences; it does not require two challenges, a one-to-one challenge/module mapping or a separate sentence per challenge. The methods block includes naming the method and its overall idea, then explaining the relevant mechanisms and their relationships as detailed below. A supplied reference abstract illustrates rhetorical functions and relationships, not a required sentence count, research mechanism or set of scientific claims.

Within **自己的方法**, use this adaptable explanation logic: **方法及核心设计思路 → 关键机制如何回应已提出的挑战 → 按真实依赖、并行或互补关系展开必要细节 → 必要补充机制的独特作用与效果**. Allocate as many sentences as the actual method and budget need. The mechanism may be a model, objective, training strategy, inference procedure or other genuine design; do not force named modules, a foundation model, sequential stages or an extra component. Introduce a remaining-problem transition only where the actual argument requires it, without claiming that one solved stage creates or transfers the core challenge. Each included detail must explain why/how the design answers the challenge, not merely list component names. Results must support the capabilities and scope described.

**Confirmed abstract length default:** target **180–210 English words**, with an upper limit of **220 words**. Explicit target-journal requirements take priority over both the default range and cap, including required length ranges, structured headings and counting conventions; verify those requirements when a journal is named. Count the readable abstract text, including a code-availability sentence if it is inside the abstract, rather than TeX comments or formatting commands. Allocate the budget among the functional blocks and then individual sentences according to actual argument/evidence needs; do not impose a universal per-sentence quota. Favorite references guide expression and allocation within the applicable budget, not an automatic exception to it. Do not pad an otherwise concise abstract with filler or invent evidence to reach the target; surface a genuine conflict with completeness or scope to the author. These are English-word defaults, not Chinese-character limits.

Orchestra's five-role abstract seed is comparative background only; source notes are consolidated in the reference guide. Derive the actual Chinese sentence plan from the author's reference, confirmed functional structure, available evidence and applicable budget, without importing conference-layout quotas.
